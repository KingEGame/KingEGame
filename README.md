### Selected work

Public links are for verification. Private platforms are summarized at a high level only.

#### Research (public proof)

##### [StoneRecognition / stone-detection-service](https://github.com/StoneRecognition/stone-detection-service)
CV research for stone detection/segmentation (YOLO + SAM-family prototypes). Org **admin**. Also public: [StoneSegment](https://github.com/StoneRecognition/StoneSegment).

##### [QWEN-vLLM-LoRA](https://github.com/KingEGame/QWEN-vLLM-LoRA)
Local Qwen serving + LoRA/QLoRA operator tooling with vLLM on NVIDIA GPU (WSL2/Linux). Personal public research — judge by guides and code, not star counts.

##### [3d-gripper-opencv](https://github.com/KingEGame/3d-gripper-opencv)
Computer vision + control path for a 3D gripper using OpenCV and a camera feed — hardware-adjacent prototype with a clear problem statement.

#### Building (partially public)

##### [audio-automation-project](https://github.com/audio-automation-project)
Audiobook / audio-library automation platform. Org **admin**. Public: [docs](https://github.com/audio-automation-project/docs), [audio-frontend](https://github.com/audio-automation-project/audio-frontend), shared CI. ML services remain **private**.

##### [health-notifier-backend](https://github.com/KingEGame/health-notifier-backend)
Personal public backend prototype — useful for reading API/service structure; not a product launch claim.

#### Building (private — sanitized case study)

Across private multi-service work I design and ship **frontends, backends, shared contracts, edge gateways, and CI/docs** for automation and product platforms (job-application automation, LMS, scheduling, garden-station R&D, and collaborative apartment-platform frontend). Public detail stays high-level: problem framing, service boundaries, and delivery practices — not private source, credentials, or confidential client material. Ask for a walkthrough.
